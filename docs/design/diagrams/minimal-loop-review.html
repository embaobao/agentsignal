AGENTSIGNAL · MILESTONE M2/M3 CHAIN REVIEW

The Simplest Loop That Proves the Product

One publisher, one watcher, one hundred envelopes. If ninety die for free and ten earn a thought, the bus works — and the review notes below check every invariant on this path.

REVIEW NOTE 01

Dedupe happens before fetch, not after

Delivery is at-least-once; a crashed watcher replays its last cursor. The contract holds because message.id dedupe sits in front of the payload fetch — repetition wastes a local check, never a token.

REVIEW NOTE 02

Clock-stable cursors make crash recovery boring

cursor = ULID id means recovery is “read last id, ask for more”. No sequence tables, no offsets, nothing to rebuild. The dotted return arrow is what makes the loop re-runnable forever.

If one hundred messages go in and ninety fall out of this chart without a model ever noticing they existed, AgentSignal has already proved its reason to exist.
